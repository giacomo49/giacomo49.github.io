Physics, athletics and programming look like three disconnected lives. In practice they resemble
each other more than I'd like to admit: all three are about doing something difficult many times,
measuring each time whether it went better.

This site is where I keep the three together. It isn't a CV and it doesn't aim to be a magazine:
these are public notes. Writing for someone who will read forces me to close the gaps that,
when I study alone, I tend to skip.

The plan is simple: publish when I've understood something, not when I've finished studying it.

If you want to follow along there's the [RSS feed](/rss.xml), or you can find me
on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/giacomoman_).
